Ten tools, one menu bar icon

Turn off any tool you don't need. It disappears from the panel and stops running.

Clean Up

Finds caches, logs and build leftovers. You review every path; items go to the Trash.

Disk Map

Shows what takes space, folder by folder. Starts only when you click.

Uninstaller

Removes an app together with its caches, preferences and login items.

Startup Items

Everything that starts with your Mac, in one list you can switch off.

Capture

Screenshots with an editor, screen recording with system audio and camera.

Clipboard

History of text, links and images. Search it and paste with ⌥⌘V.

Keep Awake

One switch to stop sleep. Can also stay awake while Claude Code is working.

Mac Health

CPU, memory, disk, network and battery, including Bluetooth devices.

Fans

Live fan speeds and temperatures, with manual control when you need it.

Claude Usage

Your Claude Code limits at a glance. Optional: Claude can review cleanup paths.

Record, then share a link

Press ⇧⌘2, pick an area, record. Cmd+Meow uploads the file to your own S3 storage and copies a link that opens in a clean player.

  • Records with system audio and an optional camera bubble
  • Your bucket, your keys. The secret stays in the Keychain
  • Links open in the Cmd+Meow player on any device

Install

Open the dmg and drag Cmd+Meow to Applications. The app isn't notarized yet, so open it once with right-click › Open, or run:

xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/CmdMeow.app

Or build it yourself: cargo run --release. Requires macOS 12 or later.
